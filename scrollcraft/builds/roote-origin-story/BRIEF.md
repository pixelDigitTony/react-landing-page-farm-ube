# Roote Origin: a harvest with a history

Implemented under the user's instruction: "implement the plan in detail". The accepted source is `design/SCROLL-STORY-PLAN.md`. Earlier briefs remain historical records.

## Creative brief

- Audience: public consumers and prospective cooperative stakeholders.
- Existing user direction: photographic Davao farm, green pipe trellis, leaf lookup, ube and cooperative. "needs to be like telling a story similar to popular scroll animation pages".
- Authored implementation: six chapters follow fictional farm 0001 and batch SAMPLE-UBE-001. Immediate lookup, farm origin, underground reveal, harvest record, processor/recipient history, cooperative and farm choices.
- Tone: grounded, cinematic, curious, clear. Preserve forest green and warm ivory.
- Structure: guided chapters, one short desktop pin, normal-flow utilities. No continuous worldflight or forced root-drop intro.
- Assets: existing photographic originals; built-in imagegen extracted a matching yam/root cluster. Blender renders a photographic 2.5D cutaway scene, using textured depth cards and an authored camera descent. This is explicitly not a full botanical 3D model or verified growing-season simulation.
- Primary action: Find origin. Narrative payoff: Open sample batch.
- Scope: static React/TypeScript frontend, Node 26 tools; future MERN system remains separate.

## Feeling curve

1. Curiosity/control: immediate landscape, headline, and working leaf lookup.
2. Connection: an expanding farm photograph names the sample origin.
3. Wonder: the camera crosses an irregular soil boundary to expose the connected yam.
4. Understanding: that same rendered yam shifts into a real batch record.
5. Clarity/trust: a root-like line follows recorded origin, harvest, processor, and intended recipient; a real QR opens the summary.
6. Confidence: the cooperative and farms are clear destinations; the final frame holds complete content.

Peak: "I followed the plant underground, then followed its harvest back to a farm and the people who handled it."

Tell someone: It is the site where the crop you discover beneath the soil becomes a public origin record you can open.

There are no empty authored holds. The last camera frames settle briefly while the heading remains available.

## Grammar and fingerprint

The organising structure is a guided harvest story. Each semantic chapter changes information and composition; the origin utility remains immediately available and all chapters can be bypassed. A filmic one-shot prevents the required jump navigation; pure editorial conflicts with the selected photographic hero; a live surface overemphasizes system UI; galleries, type-led structures, and split arguments do not explain this crop's journey. The previous worldflight was already replaced at the user's instruction.

Against current Roote Origin: structure, six-beat sequence, public-record close, and harvest-to-record signature differ. Hero identity and primary navigation are intentionally retained. Against original Ube Farm: structure, opening, navigation, sequence, close, and signature differ.
