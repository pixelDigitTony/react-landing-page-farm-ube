1. Curiosity: root suspended beside the trellis, then soil contact.
2. Wonder: vine climbs into the canopy. This is the visual peak and has the longest individual span.
3. Control: stable ID lookup and camera entry on the tip leaf.
4. Understanding: company details unfold on a leaf during descent.
5. Trust: stable growing-practice copy against a leaf surface reveal.
6. Appreciation: tactile purple yam imagery reveals at the harvest leaf.
7. Connection: linked farms arrive on the lowest leaves.
8. Confidence: the original root resolves into the leadership board and holds.

## Grammar and score

Continuous world was explicitly selected. One fixed visual stage, native scroll track, waypoint navigation, and stable semantic copy. Layered imagery, growth masks, camera translation, leaf unfolding, and root-board reveal provide variation without section boundaries or chained clips. The other seven grammars do not satisfy the requested connected physical journey.

- Filmic one-shot would organize separate cinematic acts instead of preserving the same plant throughout.
- Chaptered editorial would prioritize independent reading sections over the requested ascent and return.
- Live surface would make a changing work surface the center rather than the plant's physical journey.
- Typographic poster would make type the primary subject and reduce the botanical transformation.
- Gallery/catalog would put farm browsing before the root-to-canopy story.
- Split stage would divide the experience into competing panels instead of one connected world.
- Rhythmic cutlist would use cuts between scenes, contradicting the requested continuous plant.
