# Ube Farm revision report

The existing continuous botanical journey was rebuilt from the user's exported Figma foundations, UI components, motion board and transparent artwork. Cue (https://www.cuewigs.com/) was inspected in headless Chrome for pacing, changes of scale and expressive typography. The farm retains its own identity. This is frontend only, with static records and no API requests.

The grammar, eight-stage sequence and signature remain the accepted build. The seven alternatives do not preserve the requested connected plant, as recorded in BRIEF.md. This is a revision of the existing fingerprint row, rather than a new site.

## What changed

- One physical camera now contains the green pipe, vine, root, connected leaves, soil and root board. It replaces the separate oversized background and floating copy layers.
- The root drops into overlapping soil and sprouts. A growing helix, leaf unfurling, pullback and ascent form the longest act and the one major peak.
- The tip leaf fully opens before the finder becomes interactive. The descent settles at useful reading and form positions.
- Company copy reveals by line, practice pods arrive in sequence, harvest imagery turns independently, and partner leaves fan from their petioles.
- Stem endpoints are calculated from the actual leaf SVGs, maintaining their attachment through responsive layouts, rotation and zoom.
- The leadership board expands around the original root's anchor and holds with an actionable farm finder.
- Exported pill buttons, rounded fields, partner cards, serif italic type and purple brand mark replace the prior component approximations.
- Phones have a separate pipe position, portrait leaves, vertically stacked partners and a keyboard-sized finder layout. Reduced motion uses a normal reading document.

The contact-sheet read is an authored visual assessment, not a user feeling study. Initial screenshots exposed a partially opened tip leaf, a phone pipe crossing the headline, detached petioles, hard photographic growth edges and a shrinking soil strip. Those were corrected in this revision. Reading holds are deliberate; camera movement resumes during each act's last third.

## Evidence and limits

Node 26.9.0, full ffmpeg, Playwright and installed Chrome pass scroll-craft preflight. The optional generation key is absent; no new imagery or footage was generated. Supplied tight cutouts are encoded as WebP. Skill engine files remain unchanged.

The production build, lint and 13 unit tests pass. Production-preview browser checks sample six positions per stage plus twelve overview positions at 1440×900, 390×844 and 360×640. They check leaf attachment within two pixels, fully opened leaves, opaque copy, viewport fit, horizontal overflow, ID errors and leading zeroes, static partner/profile routes, Back restoration, downloaded QR decoding, reduced motion, keyboard resize and scanner recovery. Real ZXing decoding is exercised against a simulated camera stream. Screenshots and exact results are in verification/report.json and the contact sheets.

The exports contain component and motion reference boards, not complete desktop/mobile screens, so full-screen Figma parity is not claimed. Physical-device touch scrolling, actual camera capture, browser chrome and real keyboard behavior remain unverified. No deployment was performed.
