01 / INCLUDED DELIVERABLES
What the MVP includes
Help visitors discover the cooperative's farms and produce, give authorized ecosystem members
access to connected batch records, and let consumers scan a public summary for the specific
batch behind a partner product.
01 Animated landing page Retain the root drop, green trellis, growing vine, tip-leaf lookup, descending
stories, partner leaves and root leadership board. Include mobile layouts, skip-
intro and reduced-motion reading.
02 Farm & produce inventory Search and filter approved farms and their produce under Roote Origin. Show
public profiles, crop/variety, harvest or availability information and published
batch links. Start with ube; the data model allows other produce.
03 Ecosystem accounts &
roles
Invite/login, password recovery and a member dashboard for administrators,
growers, processors, brand owners and distributors. Authorized members can
read the full permitted batch history; editing is limited by role and assignment.
04 Farming & harvest records Unique batch ID; farm, plot/site, grower, produce/variety, planting date, agreed
farming activities and input notes; harvest date, quantity and unit. Include
validation, status and a dated edit history.
05 Processor & recipient links Record who processed the batch, when and where, link the source harvest batch
to its processed lot, and identify the intended brand owner or distributor.
Preserve the origin chain for authorized members.
06 Public per-batch QR Preview and publish an approved consumer summary on a stable batch URL.
Download QR artwork in PNG/SVG for partner cafes, restaurants or packaging.
Scanning or entering a batch ID opens that specific summary.
07 Admin editing & pilot
setup
Manage farms, produce, partners, users, batches, public visibility and manually
recorded availability. Assisted setup for up to 10 farms, 20 batches and 25
accounts; normal self-service records can be added afterward.
08 Quality, launch &
handover
Responsive and role-access testing; HTTPS deployment; basic public-page SEO;
database backup setup and one restore check; source, documentation, one
training session and 30-day in-scope defect support.
MERN stack: MongoDB database, Express API on Node.js 26 and React frontend; TypeScript across frontend/backend. Verify the
runtime and hosted MongoDB, authentication and storage plans at kickoff. One cooperative ecosystem, English and one
production deployment.
Easy content and color changes
Interface text and colors stay in a centralized constants file. Operational farm, produce and batch data is edited
through the protected dashboard and stored in the database.

Protected ecosystem data
Member view: agreed farming details, harvest and processing data, linked recipients and record history. Server-side
permission checks restrict access by role and batch assignment; the cooperative approves the visibility rules in Week
1.
Approved public QR summary
Consumer view: batch ID, produce, farm/location, approved growing summary, harvest date, processor and
brand/distributor names where approved. Personal contacts, internal notes and commercial information remain
private unless explicitly approved for publication.
Two kinds of discovery
Farm lookup and farm QR open a farm profile. Batch lookup and batch QR open a batch summary. Administrators can unpublish a
summary without deleting its private history. A stable URL keeps printed QR artwork usable after approved content updates;
physical printing is excluded.
The MVP records information supplied by ecosystem participants. QR traceability does not independently certify authenticity,
organic status, export eligibility or regulatory compliance. The initial workflow supports one source harvest batch per processed
lot; mixed-origin blending and complex split/merge accounting require separate scope.

04 / WEEKLY DELIVERY PLAN
Ten weeks from kickoff to launch
Target: 10 working weeks after written scope approval, complete kickoff inputs and initial payment.
Weeks are sequential delivery targets, not fixed calendar dates. Client review, missing data or
approved scope changes may extend the schedule.
WEEK MILESTONE PLANNED OUTPUT
Week 1 Confirm the system Approve fields, roles, public/private rules, pilot data, one design
direction and the batch workflow.
Week 2 Build the foundation Set up MongoDB, the Express/Node.js API, authentication,
environments and server-side access rules.
Week 3 Organize the ecosystem Build farm, produce, partner and account management; prepare the
public inventory structure.
Week 4 Capture farming details Implement planting, plot/site, care/input notes and authorized record
editing.
Week 5 Track harvest batches Implement batch IDs, harvest dates, quantities, availability/status and
record history.
Week 6 Connect processing & recipients Link processors, processed lots, brand owners and distributors to
their source batches.
Week 7 Publish the consumer view Build approved batch summaries, publication controls, stable URLs
and QR downloads.
Week 8 Connect the landing experience Integrate live public inventory, lookup and batch links; refine mobile
and scroll behavior.
Week 9 Review & accept the pilot Run workflow, role, QR and device checks; complete two consolidated
in-scope revision rounds.
Week 10 Launch & transfer Configure HTTPS/backups, check restore, train operators, hand over
source and start defect support.
Review cadence
Review working features on staging at each milestone. Consolidated client feedback is requested within two
working days. Week 9 includes the agreed pilot review and two revision rounds; approved changes may shift
Week 10.

05 / OPERATING BUDGET & BOUNDARIES
Keep the pilot focused
Development, running costs and future enhancements are agreed separately.
Operating costs, paid separately
Operating allowance: PHP 2,500-5,000/month for a small Node.js/Express API, MongoDB hosting, authentication/file
storage, backups and essential service usage; domain PHP 1,000-3,500/year. These are planning allowances, not
provider quotations. Client-owned accounts pay actual charges; traffic, storage, email usage, exchange rates and plan
changes affect cost.
First-year base budget: ₱ 251,000- ₱ 283,500
Project fee + 12 months of operating allowance + domain. Excludes optional maintenance, premium domains, paid asset
licenses, excess provider usage and applicable taxes. Any applicable tax treatment will be stated in the final
quotation/invoice.
Optional ongoing maintenance
Optional maintenance: PHP 4,000/month for up to four hours of routine checks, minor fixes/updates and support. New
features and extra hours are separately quoted. This service is not automatically billed and is separate from the
included 30-day defect support.
Meaning of inventory in this MVP
Inventory means a farm/produce catalogue with recorded batch quantities and manually maintained availability. It
does not include a full warehouse ledger, reservations, sales orders, POS, invoicing or real-time stock
synchronization.
