01 / Included deliverables

What the MVP includes

Help visitors discover the cooperative's farms and produce, give authorized ecosystem members access to connected batch records, and let consumers scan a public summary for the specific batch behind a partner product.

01Animated landing page

Retain the root drop, green trellis, growing vine, tip-leaf lookup, descending stories, partner leaves and root leadership board. Include mobile layouts, skip-intro and reduced-motion reading.

02Farm & produce inventory

Search and filter approved farms and their produce under Roote Origin. Show public profiles, crop/variety, harvest or availability information and published batch links. Start with ube; the data model allows other produce.

03Ecosystem accounts & roles

Invite/login, password recovery and a member dashboard for administrators, growers, processors, brand owners and distributors. Authorized members can read the full permitted batch history; editing is limited by role and assignment.

04Farming & harvest records

Unique batch ID; farm, plot/site, grower, produce/variety, planting date, agreed farming activities and input notes; harvest date, quantity and unit. Include validation, status and a dated edit history.

05Processor & recipient links

Record who processed the batch, when and where, link the source harvest batch to its processed lot, and identify the intended brand owner or distributor. Preserve the origin chain for authorized members.

06Public per-batch QR

Preview and publish an approved consumer summary on a stable batch URL. Download QR artwork in PNG/SVG for partner cafes, restaurants or packaging. Scanning or entering a batch ID opens that specific summary.

07Admin editing & pilot setup

Manage farms, produce, partners, users, batches, public visibility and manually recorded availability. Assisted setup for up to 10 farms, 20 batches and 25 accounts; normal self-service records can be added afterward.

08Quality, launch & handover

Responsive and role-access testing; HTTPS deployment; basic public-page SEO; database backup setup and one restore check; source, documentation, one training session and 30-day in-scope defect support.

MERN stack: MongoDB database, Express API on Node.js 26 and React frontend; TypeScript across frontend/backend. Verify the runtime and hosted MongoDB, authentication and storage plans at kickoff. One cooperative ecosystem, English and one production deployment.

Easy content and color changes

Interface text and colors stay in a centralized constants file. Operational farm, produce and batch data is edited through the protected dashboard and stored in the database.

Protected ecosystem data

Member view: agreed farming details, harvest and processing data, linked recipients and record history. Server-side permission checks restrict access by role and batch assignment; the cooperative approves the visibility rules in Week 1.

Approved public QR summary

Consumer view: batch ID, produce, farm/location, approved growing summary, harvest date, processor and brand/distributor names where approved. Personal contacts, internal notes and commercial information remain private unless explicitly approved for publication.

Two kinds of discovery

Farm lookup and farm QR open a farm profile. Batch lookup and batch QR open a batch summary. Administrators can unpublish a summary without deleting its private history. A stable URL keeps printed QR artwork usable after approved content updates; physical printing is excluded.

The MVP records information supplied by ecosystem participants. QR traceability does not independently certify authenticity, organic status, export eligibility or regulatory compliance. The initial workflow supports one source harvest batch per processed lot; mixed-origin blending and complex split/merge accounting require separate scope.

First-year base budget: ₱251,000-₱283,500

Project fee + 12 months of operating allowance + domain. Excludes optional maintenance, premium domains, paid asset licenses, excess provider usage and applicable taxes. Any applicable tax treatment will be stated in the final quotation/invoice.

Optional ongoing maintenance

Optional maintenance: PHP 4,000/month for up to four hours of routine checks, minor fixes/updates and support. New features and extra hours are separately quoted. This service is not automatically billed and is separate from the included 30-day defect support.

Meaning of inventory in this MVP

Inventory means a farm/produce catalogue with recorded batch quantities and manually maintained availability. It does not include a full warehouse ledger, reservations, sales orders, POS, invoicing or real-time stock synchronization.

Outside the initial MVP

E-commerce, payments, POS, accounting, shipment tracking and full warehouse/ERP stock control.

06 / Acceptance & agreement

Ready to pilot, clear to approve

Acceptance before launch

  1. An authorized grower, processor and recipient can complete the agreed batch journey; linked records persist after refresh and re-login.
  2. Public inventory shows only approved farms/produce and batch links. Farm IDs/links and batch IDs/QRs resolve to their respective pages.
  3. Logged-out and unauthorized API requests cannot read private or unassigned records; role-restricted edits and publication controls are enforced.
  4. A production QR opens the correct published summary without login; unpublished/unknown IDs and denied camera access have clear recovery states.
  5. Mobile, keyboard and reduced-motion views work; Android/Chrome and iPhone/Safari QR checks, backup restore and operator handover are completed.

Client inputs & pilot support

Provide the approved cooperative identity/photos, Sir Marco's official roles, pilot farm/produce/batch records, processor and brand/distributor details, role assignments and public-field consent. Confirm the workflow and data visibility, supply client-owned service accounts, and return consolidated feedback within two working days of each review.

Assisted onboarding covers up to 10 farms, 20 batches and 25 accounts. These are setup allowances, not software record limits. Creating ordinary records through delivered forms is included; bulk migration, developer-assisted entry beyond the allowance or new record types are separately negotiated.

Two consolidated revision rounds refine the agreed MVP. A new design concept or changed workflow is a scope change. The 30-day defect-support period starts at launch and covers defects in delivered features; ongoing data entry, new features, provider outages and 24/7 incident response are excluded.
