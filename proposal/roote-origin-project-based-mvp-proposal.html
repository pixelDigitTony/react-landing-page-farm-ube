01 / Deliverables

One design, one connected MVP

Help visitors discover the cooperative's farms and produce, give authorized ecosystem members access to connected batch records, and let consumers scan a public summary for the specific batch behind a partner product.

01One public design

Implement one approved direction from the three concepts. Include its agreed scroll story, responsive mobile composition, accessible navigation and reduced-motion view. Reuse the current prototype and artwork where suitable.

02Farm & produce catalogue

Search farms and produce; filter the catalogue by crop/location; open approved farm profiles, availability and published batch links. Begin with ube; ordinary produce records can be added later.

03Protected member portal

Login, password recovery and assigned member dashboards. Administrators, growers, processors and brand owners/distributors see permitted batch history; backend rules restrict edits and record access.

04Growing & harvest history

Farm, plot/site, grower, produce/variety, planting date and agreed care/input notes. Add harvest ID, date, quantity/unit and status, with validation and dated correction history.

05Processing & recipients

Record the processor, date/place, processed lot and intended brand owner or distributor. Link one source harvest batch to each processed lot and preserve its origin history.

06Public batch QR

Admin preview, approval, publish/unpublish and a stable public URL per batch. Farm/batch ID lookup, camera scanning and PNG/SVG QR downloads; public consumers need no login.

07Admin & pilot onboarding

Manage farms, produce, partners, accounts, batch records and publication. Assisted entry of up to 10 farms, 20 batches and 25 accounts; ordinary records remain self-service afterward.

08Test, launch & handover

Feature and permission testing, two review rounds, HTTPS production deployment, public-page SEO basics, backups and one restore check. Source/documentation, one training session and 30-day defect support.

MERN: MongoDB, Express API, React and Node.js 26; TypeScript across frontend and backend. One cooperative ecosystem, English, one staging environment and one production deployment. Agree hosting, storage, email and backup retention at kickoff.

Editable copy, colors and operational data

Interface text and colors stay in a centralized constants file. Operational farm, produce and batch data is edited through the protected dashboard and stored in the database.

Private member records

Member view: agreed farming details, harvest and processing data, linked recipients and record history. Server-side permission checks restrict access by role and batch assignment; the cooperative approves the visibility rules in Week 1.

Approved public summary

Consumer view: batch ID, produce, farm/location, approved growing summary, harvest date, processor and brand/distributor names where approved. Personal contacts, internal notes and commercial information remain private unless explicitly approved for publication.

Farm QR and batch QR serve different purposes

Farm lookup and farm QR open a farm profile. Batch lookup and batch QR open a batch summary. Administrators can unpublish a summary without deleting its private history. A stable URL keeps printed QR artwork usable after approved content updates; physical printing is excluded. Partners display the appropriate batch QR and replace it when the batch changes.

The MVP records information supplied by ecosystem participants. QR traceability does not independently certify authenticity, organic status, export eligibility or regulatory compliance. The initial workflow supports one source harvest batch per processed lot; mixed-origin blending and complex split/merge accounting require separate scope.

What inventory means here

Inventory means a farm/produce catalogue with recorded batch quantities and manually maintained availability. It does not include a full warehouse ledger, reservations, sales orders, POS, invoicing or real-time stock synchronization.

Outside this project fee

E-commerce, payments, POS, accounting, shipment tracking and full warehouse/ERP stock control.

Mixed-origin batch blending, complex split/merge reconciliation and automated stock reservations.

Existing-system integrations, bulk legacy migration, IoT/sensor feeds and offline/native apps.

Blockchain, independent certification, export compliance verification and advanced analytics.

Independent multi-tenant organizations, extra languages, major new 3D scenes and media production.

Single-source batch workflow

The quoted pilot assumes one source harvest batch per processed lot. If processors combine harvests or farms, revise the data workflow, cost and delivery plan before commitment. Physical QR printing, professional photography and paid asset licenses are excluded.

06 / Pilot acceptance and operating costs

Ready to run and ready to hand over

Acceptance checks

  1. Assigned grower, processor and recipient complete the agreed farming-to-recipient flow; linked records persist after refresh and re-login.
  2. Logged-out and unauthorized API requests cannot read private/unassigned records or perform restricted edits; only administrators publish.
  3. Only approved farm/produce pages and public batch links appear. ID lookup and production QR open the correct farm or batch; unpublished/unknown records have recovery states.
  4. The selected design works on mobile, keyboard and reduced motion. Android/Chrome and iPhone/Safari camera checks are completed with agreed test devices.
  5. HTTPS launch, backup restore check, operator training, source files and editing/deployment documentation are completed.

Client inputs and assisted onboarding

Supply approved identity/photos and leadership roles, pilot farm/produce/batch data, processor/recipient details, account assignments and public-field approval. Confirm whether harvests are combined, provide client-owned deployment accounts, and assign one decision-maker for consolidated feedback.

Assisted onboarding covers up to 10 farms, 20 batches and 25 accounts. These are setup allowances, not software record limits. Creating ordinary records through delivered forms is included; bulk migration, developer-assisted entry beyond the allowance or new record types are separately negotiated.

Operating costs paid separately

Operating allowance: PHP 2,500-5,000/month for a small Node.js/Express API, MongoDB hosting, authentication/file storage, backups and essential service usage; domain PHP 1,000-3,500/year. These are planning allowances, not provider quotations. Client-owned accounts pay actual charges; traffic, storage, email usage, exchange rates and plan changes affect cost.

First-year base allowance: ₱251,000-₱283,500

Project fee + 12 months of operating allowance + domain. Excludes optional maintenance, premium domains, paid asset licenses, excess provider usage and applicable taxes. Any applicable tax treatment will be stated in the final quotation/invoice.

Calculation: ₱220,000 + (₱2,500-₱5,000) x 12 months + ₱1,000-₱3,500 domain/year.

Optional maintenance

Optional maintenance: PHP 4,000/month for up to four hours of routine checks, minor fixes/updates and support. New features and extra hours are separately quoted. This service is not automatically billed and is separate from the included 30-day defect support.

07 / Proposed project-based terms

Clear delivery and handover

These arrangements apply only if project-based delivery is selected and the final agreement is approved.

Engagement and review

Delivery covers the defined MVP through acceptance, launch, handover and 30-day defect support. Weekly progress updates and scheduled milestone reviews are included. Ongoing operations, daily staff duties, open-ended development and employment availability are outside this project fee.

Two consolidated revision rounds refine the approved design and workflow. Report reproducible acceptance issues against the agreed criteria; in-scope defects are corrected before acceptance. Late feedback moves dependent milestones and does not by itself constitute acceptance.

Source, accounts and data

After full payment, deliver the agreed custom source code and documentation to client-controlled accounts; record ownership/licensing terms in the final agreement. Third-party tools, fonts and assets remain subject to their licenses. The cooperative owns its operational data and is responsible for its accuracy and approved publication.

Included launch support

30 days from production launch for defects in delivered functionality. No automatic maintenance subscription. Daily data entry, new features, third-party incidents and 24/7 response are excluded; a separate support arrangement can be agreed after the pilot.

Costing is negotiable

The PHP 220,000 proposed project fee, milestone percentages and payment terms are negotiable. Final costing, scope, selected design, working availability and launch schedule will be mutually confirmed in writing before development starts.

Additional features incur additional cost

Additional features, integrations, new animation scenes, major redesigns or expanded workflows will incur additional cost depending on complexity and negotiations. Approve a written change request with its deliverables, additional fee and timeline impact before that work begins.
