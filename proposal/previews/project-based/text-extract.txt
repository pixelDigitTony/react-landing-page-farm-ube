behind a partner product.

Private member records
Member view: agreed farming details, harvest and processing data, linked recipients and record history. Server-side
permission checks restrict access by role and batch assignment; the cooperative approves the visibility rules in Week
1.
Approved public summary
Consumer view: batch ID, produce, farm/location, approved growing summary, harvest date, processor and
brand/distributor names where approved. Personal contacts, internal notes and commercial information remain private
unless explicitly approved for publication.
Farm QR and batch QR serve different purposes
Farm lookup and farm QR open a farm profile. Batch lookup and batch QR open a batch summary. Administrators can unpublish
a summary without deleting its private history. A stable URL keeps printed QR artwork usable after approved content updates;
physical printing is excluded. Partners display the appropriate batch QR and replace it when the batch changes.
The MVP records information supplied by ecosystem participants. QR traceability does not independently certify authenticity,
organic status, export eligibility or regulatory compliance. The initial workflow supports one source harvest batch per processed
lot; mixed-origin blending and complex split/merge accounting require separate scope.

07 / PROPOSED PROJECT-BASED TERMS
Clear delivery and handover
These arrangements apply only if project-based delivery is selected and the final agreement is
approved.
Engagement and review
Delivery covers the defined MVP through acceptance, launch, handover and 30-day defect support. Weekly progress
updates and scheduled milestone reviews are included. Ongoing operations, daily staff duties, open-ended
development and employment availability are outside this project fee.
Two consolidated revision rounds refine the approved design and workflow. Report reproducible acceptance issues
against the agreed criteria; in-scope defects are corrected before acceptance. Late feedback moves dependent
milestones and does not by itself constitute acceptance.
Source, accounts and data
After full payment, deliver the agreed custom source code and documentation to client-controlled accounts; record
ownership/licensing terms in the final agreement. Third-party tools, fonts and assets remain subject to their licenses.
The cooperative owns its operational data and is responsible for its accuracy and approved publication.
Included launch support
30 days from production launch for defects in delivered functionality. No automatic maintenance subscription. Daily
data entry, new features, third-party incidents and 24/7 response are excluded; a separate support arrangement can
be agreed after the pilot.
Costing is negotiable
The PHP 220,000 proposed project fee, milestone percentages and payment terms are negotiable. Final costing,
scope, selected design, working availability and launch schedule will be mutually confirmed in writing before
development starts.
Additional features incur additional cost
Additional features, integrations, new animation scenes, major redesigns or expanded workflows will incur additional
cost depending on complexity and negotiations. Approve a written change request with its deliverables, additional
fee and timeline impact before that work begins.
